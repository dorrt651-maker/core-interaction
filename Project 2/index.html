How to Draw a Stick Figure

Follow these steps to draw a simple stick figure:

  1. Locate the closest pen and paper.
  2. Pick up the pen and hold it in a comfortable grip for drawing.
  3. Locate a few centimeters above the center of the paper.
  4. Draw a circle with no more than 4 inches in diameter for the head.
  5. Draw a line that comes down from bottom, center of the head for the body. This line should be about twice the height of the head.
  6. Locate the center of that line and draw a diagonal line going towards the left side of the page. This line should be about half the size of the body line.
  7. Repeat the previous step, but this time draw a diagonal line going towards the right side of the page.
  8. Add 2 diagonal lines at the bottom of the body. They should be the same size as the other diagonal lines. The lines should create about a 45 to 60 degree angle, that opens facing the bottom of the page.
  9. You can then add details like facial features in the cirlce.
  10. You can then place your pen down, after you are satisfied witht the drawing.
